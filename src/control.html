Connecting to TipPhone.

  1. Connect your TipPage

    Signs you in at auth.tippage.com and asks for two permissions: read the TTS queue, and mark a tip as playing or played.

    Stays connected until you press Disconnect here or remove it in the dashboard under Settings, Developer, Connected apps.

  2. Start

    Chrome only plays sound and lists audio outputs on a page that's been clicked. If it asks for the microphone, allow it. The output names come with that permission, and nothing is recorded.

  3. Connect the phone

    Plug the phone in by USB, press Connect phone and pick it from the list that pops up. Close other applications that could be using the phone (Teams etc) first.

    No phone connected.

  4. Choose where the sound goes

    The call plays through the phone, and through whatever OBS captures as desktop audio if you want viewers to hear it. Play the test sound and check both.

    The built-in ring

  5. Show calls on streamoptional

    An OBS overlay that shows who's calling, then the amount and message while they're on the line.

    Set it up
    1. In OBS, under Sources, press + and choose Browser.
    2. Call it TipPhone and press OK.
    3. Put this in the URL box: http://127.0.0.1:4455/overlay
    4. Set Width to 1920 and Height to 1080, then OK. Size it to your canvas like any other source.
    seconds

    The status above says when OBS has loaded it. A browser tab counts as open, not as OBS.

  6. Turn tip alerts off on your alert box

    Otherwise the alert box plays every tip before the phone can ring. In the dashboard open Overlays, edit the overlay with the alert box, and under Alert events untick Tip. Leave the rest on.

    Open my overlays

    While an alert is on screen the phone holds its ring, and the alert box holds its alerts while someone is on the phone.

  7. Try a call

    Rings the phone with the last played tip that has audio. The queue isn't touched. To try the whole thing, add a manual tip from the dashboard. It joins the queue like a real tip and doesn't count as money.
